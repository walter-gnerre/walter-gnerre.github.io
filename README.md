# Walter Gnerre: Engineering Portfolio

A single-page portfolio site for GitHub Pages. No build step, no frameworks: just `index.html` and an `assets/` folder.

## Put it online (about 10 minutes, no coding)

1. Sign in to GitHub and click **New repository**.
2. Name it exactly **`your-username.github.io`** (use your real GitHub username). Make it **Public**. Click **Create repository**.
3. On the new repo page, click **uploading an existing file**.
4. Drag in everything from this folder: `index.html`, `README.md`, `.nojekyll`, and the whole `assets` folder. Click **Commit changes**.
   - `.nojekyll` is a hidden file. If your computer won't show it, skip it; the site works without it.
5. Go to **Settings → Pages**. Under **Source**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, pick **main** and **/ (root)**, and click **Save**.
6. Wait 1–2 minutes, then open **https://your-username.github.io**.

If you name the repository something else (say `portfolio`), the address becomes `https://your-username.github.io/portfolio/` instead.

## Photos

Your project photos are already in `assets/`, cropped and with their location (GPS) data removed. Two slots in **Other Builds** are still empty and stay hidden until you add a file with these exact names:

| File | Photo of |
|---|---|
| `l2-airframe.jpg` | The fiberglass Level 2 airframe |
| `raptor-v3.jpg` | A SolidWorks render of the Raptor V3 model |

Landscape works best; keep each under about 500 KB. On GitHub, open the `assets` folder, then **Add file → Upload files**. The photo appears on the page automatically.

## Edit text later

Open `index.html` on GitHub, click the pencil icon, change the words, and click **Commit changes**. The site updates within a minute or two.

To update your resume, upload a new PDF named exactly `assets/Walter_Gnerre_Resume.pdf`. It replaces the old one.

## Before you share the link

- **Your resume PDF includes your phone number**, and anything on this site is public to the whole internet. If you'd rather not publish it, upload a copy of the resume without the phone number.
- Once it's live, put the link in your LinkedIn profile (Contact info and the Featured section), your resume header, and your email signature.
